## 🏢 The Board of Directors & Strategic Cadence

The Executive Team answers directly to the Board of Directors, chaired by the CEO.

### **The Daily Board Review & Continuous Improvement**
To maintain start-up velocity and avoid corporate bloat, the Board operates on a **Daily Commit & Review** cycle:
*   **The Mechanism:** Every active development day, the executive architects and the AI agent push updates, code, and metrics directly to the GitHub repository.
*   **The Review:** The Board reviews the daily diffs, pull requests, automated test reports, and new entries in `LESSONS_LEARNED.md`.
*   **The Output:** Immediate, data-driven decisions on GTM adjustments, strategic bets, security posture, and **Continuous Evolution** (turning documented failures directly into new development guidelines or automated tests).
